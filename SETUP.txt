SETUP - make QR scanning work on other phones + see reports on your laptop
==========================================================================
You need 2 free things: (A) a Google Sheet as the database, (B) free website hosting.

A) DATABASE (Google Sheet + Apps Script)  ~5 minutes
1. Go to sheets.google.com and create a new blank spreadsheet. Name it "EcoCampus Reports".
2. Menu: Extensions > Apps Script.
3. Delete everything there. Open Code.gs from this folder in Notepad, copy ALL, paste it in.
4. Change  'change-this-password'  to your own admin password. Click Save.
5. Click Deploy > New deployment > gear icon > Web app.
   - Execute as: Me
   - Who has access: Anyone
   Click Deploy and allow permissions (Advanced > Go to project > Allow).
6. Copy the Web app URL (ends with /exec).
7. Open config.js in Notepad and paste it:
   window.ECO_API_URL = 'https://script.google.com/macros/s/XXXX/exec';
   Save.
(If you edit Code.gs later: Deploy > Manage deployments > Edit > New version > Deploy.)

B) HOSTING (needs HTTPS so phones can open it)  ~3 minutes
Easiest: Netlify Drop
1. Go to app.netlify.com/drop
2. Drag this whole QR_EcoCampus_V2 folder onto the page.
3. You get a link like https://your-name.netlify.app  (you can rename it in Site settings).
(GitHub Pages also works.)

C) USE IT
1. On your laptop open  https://your-name.netlify.app/qr-codes.html
   (open it from the hosted link, NOT from the folder on your computer).
2. Pick College Office or College Toilet, click Download QR (PNG), print it.
3. Anyone scans it -> report form opens with that location already selected -> Submit.
4. On your laptop open Issue Tracking (or Dashboard). Enter your admin password.
   - New reports appear (page refreshes by itself every 30 seconds).
   - Click Update to change status / add a resolution note.
   - Reports are also visible directly in your Google Sheet.

NOTES
- If you change the hosting link later, download the QR codes again.
- Only people with the admin password can view/update/clear reports. Anyone can submit.
- Leave config.js empty to go back to the old single-browser demo mode.
